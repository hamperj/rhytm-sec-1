DAW · USB

MIDI clock
Over USB only. DAW Start: from the top. Playing from a later point (song position + Continue): from that point. Stop stops. No clock for ½ s: back to RHYTM-SEC-1's own tempo.
USB inputs
With the stems4 firmware:
1–2 Master (stereo)3 Bass4 Kick5 Snare + clap6 Hats + perc

All USB audio is at full level whatever the MASTER knob says: turn MASTER down to silence the speaker and keep recording. Stems follow mutes and the DJ filter. Settings and the current pattern are saved once RHYTM-SEC-1 has been stopped and left alone for 10 s, and when you leave it with HOME.
